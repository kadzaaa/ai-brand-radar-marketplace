# Safety and integrity

## Claims

- Verify pricing, limits, availability, security, privacy, legal status, integrations, and product capabilities from current first-party sources.
- Mark brand-provided claims separately from independently supported claims.
- Do not invent testimonials, awards, customers, statistics, certifications, citations, or comparison results.
- Do not present an unverified claim as false; use `unverifiable` or `ambiguous`.

## Competitors and copyright

- Analyze competitor structure, facts, and citation patterns without copying protected expression.
- Paraphrase short evidence and link to sources.
- Recommend honest decision guides, not disguised attack pages.
- Preserve material disadvantages of the target brand when they affect user fit.

## Measurement integrity

- Never claim access to global Claude analytics, internal ranking, model logs, consumer conversations, or prompt volume.
- Never equate search-engine ranking, crawler visits, citations, clicks, and conversions.
- Label manual scoring, public proxies, small samples, and incomplete scans.
- Do not promise that content will be cited or recommended.

## Privacy and authorization

- Analyze public pages or data the user is authorized to provide.
- Do not request raw passwords, API keys, session cookies, private chat logs, or unnecessary personal data.
- Minimize personal information in saved scans and reports.

## Regulated and high-risk categories

Limit work to factual, compliance-conscious analysis when a category is regulated or high risk. Do not generate deceptive, unlawful, exploitative, or policy-violating promotion. Identify jurisdictional uncertainty and request qualified review when legal claims or advertising restrictions materially affect the recommendation.
